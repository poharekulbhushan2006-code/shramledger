The Market Challenge

The 450 Million Gap in Bharat's Informal Economy

92% of India's labor force works outside the formal payroll system with zero verifiable documentation.

💸
Cash-in-Hand Invisible

Wages paid in daily physical cash or uncatalogued UPI transfers leave zero digital footprint.

  • No salary slips or appointment letters
  • Instantly rejected for prime bank loans & credit cards
  • Invisible to institutional NBFC credit models
⚠️
Predatory Moneylenders

Emergency liquidity needs push migrant families into local informal moneylenders.

  • Exorbitant 36% to 60% APR compound interest rates
  • Generational debt traps triggered by monsoon illness
  • Banks unable to lend despite huge Priority Sector Lending targets

Welfare Cess Leakage

Billions in Building & Other Construction Worker (BOCW) welfare funds sit unallocated.

  • Workers cannot prove 90-day continuous site requirement
  • Fake contractor claims cause massive fraudulent drain
  • Genuine construction laborers miss out on safety nets

Cryptographic Integrity

SHA-256 Merkle DAG: Mathematical Trust

Eliminating fake certificates and backdated wage tampering through cryptographic certainty.

🌳
Merkle Tree Architecture

Individual daily work entries form cryptographic leaves. Leaves are paired and hashed recursively into an immutable Merkle Root.

  • Canonical JSON deterministic hashing prevents formatting anomalies
  • O(log N) verification: Lenders verify any single day without loading entire history
  • Zero reliance on third-party verification intermediaries
Root: 9e8a7b6c5d4e3f2a109876543210fedcba9876543210abcdef0123456789abcd
⚡
Interactive Tamper Simulation Sandbox

Demonstrated live in the website's Merkle DAG Visualizer. Change even a single number, and the entire cryptographic chain breaks.

  • Simulate altering ₹850 to ₹1,850 on an old entry
  • Immediate leaf rehash cascades upwards into root mismatch
  • System flags: TAMPER_DETECTED: Cryptographic Hash Mismatch

Data Governance

DPDP Act 2023: Worker-First Consent Engine

Strict alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, keeping the worker in total control.

📋
Purpose-Bound Access

Lenders cannot access a worker's ledger without an explicit, time-bound consent grant.

  • Purposes: Loan Underwriting, Welfare Grant, Job Match
  • Worker selects exact data fields to share
  • Logged in immutable audit trail
⏳
Time-To-Live (TTL) Expiration

Access tokens automatically expire after the designated underwriting window.

  • Default 7-day or 30-day evaluation window
  • Prevents perpetual background data harvesting
  • Stops unapproved data sharing across affiliates
🚫
1-Click Instant Revocation

Workers can revoke consent at any moment with a single tap in their dashboard.

  • Immediately invalidates NBFC API credentials
  • Aadhaar & UAN numbers masked by default (XXXX-XXXX-4829)
  • Full compliance with DPDP Right to Erasure
Underwriting Intelligence

ShramScore™: Multi-Factor Informal Credit

A sophisticated alternative underwriting model turning informal work history into loan approvals.

🎯
5 Core Algorithmic Parameters
  • Wage Consistency (25%): Standard deviation of weekly income over 90 days.
  • Work Continuity (25%): Ratio of active working days (benchmark: 22+ days/mo).
  • Employer Endorsement (20%): Ratio of entries verified by contractors via OTP.
  • Digital Trail Ratio (15%): Proportion of payments received via bank/UPI.
  • Evidence Strength (15%): Platinum (cryptographic), Gold, Silver, Bronze.
